Featured Project

Final Year Project

Upgradation of a Dual-Extruder 3D Printer

HITEC University, Taxila · Supervisor: Dr. Muhammad Farhan Ausaf

Objective: improve structural rigidity, print accuracy, and functionality of an existing printer platform through a redesigned frame, upgraded extrusion system, and improved motion control.

  • Redesigned the printer frame for improved structural rigidity and dimensional stability, validating improvements through structural and modal analysis in ANSYS.
  • Engineered a 2-in-1 dual-extruder upgrade and a linear rail motion system to improve print accuracy and reliability.
  • Integrated Klipper firmware to improve motion control and print performance.
  • Authored the FYP thesis, documenting design methodology and simulation results chapter by chapter.
  • Delivered the completed printer to HITEC University as a donated laboratory asset for ongoing student use.
TOOLS — SolidWorks · ANSYS (structural & modal) · Klipper firmware
